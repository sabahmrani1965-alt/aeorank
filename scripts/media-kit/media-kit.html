Our process

1

Find the threads that matter

We search Reddit for the questions buyers ask in the brand’s category and keep the busiest, most-read threads, including older ones that still rank and still feed AI answers.

2

Read before writing

We read the whole thread: what was asked, which tools people recommend and why, and what the subreddit’s rules allow.

3

Write a reply worth upvoting on its own

Each reply answers the question first, compares honestly, and mentions the brand only where it genuinely fits. No sales copy, no link dropping.

4

The brand approves every word

Nothing is posted without the brand’s approval. They can edit, reject or post it themselves.

5

Published by real Reddit users

Approved replies are published from the brand’s own account or by established Reddit users in our network, following each subreddit’s rules.

6

Measure the effect

We track each reply on Reddit and the AI answers for the brand’s prompts, so the brand sees whether it is being named more often.

What we do

  • Helpful answers to real questions
  • Honest comparisons, including competitors
  • Every reply approved by the brand
  • Each subreddit’s rules followed

What we never do

  • Bought votes or fake engagement
  • Fake reviews or impersonation
  • Bots or mass-posted spam
  • Links dropped where they don’t help
