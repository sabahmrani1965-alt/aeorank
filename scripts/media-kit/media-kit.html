We get SaaS brands named in AI answers.

When a buyer asks ChatGPT “what is the best CRM for a small business?”, the answer names three to six products. Most brands don’t know whether they are among them. AEOrank measures it every day across ChatGPT, Gemini and Claude, finds the sources those answers are built from, and helps the brand get into them, starting with Reddit.

How it works

1

Measure

We ask ChatGPT, Gemini and Claude the questions a brand’s buyers ask, every day: is the brand named, in what position, who is named instead, and which pages ChatGPT cites as sources.

2

Find the gaps

AI assistants lean on Reddit threads and review pages. AEOrank finds the threads where buyers ask for tools in the category, and which competitors those threads name.

3

Act

AEOrank drafts helpful, non-promotional replies. The brand approves each one, then publishes it or has our posting network publish it, and the tracker shows whether the answers change.
