## Medium: /llms.txt returns 404
Worth stating honestly: llms.txt is not consumed by Google Search, and neither
OpenAI nor Perplexity have committed to it. Its value here is positioning —
an AEO vendor without one is a question a prospect may ask — not ranking.

## The H1 problem is also an AI problem
Answer engines lean on document outline to extract passages. 31 pages that
begin at h2 with no h1 give every extractor a weaker signal about what the
page is actually about.
