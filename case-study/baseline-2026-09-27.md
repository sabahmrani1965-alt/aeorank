# AEO visibility study — baseline
Subjects: AEOrank and SaaSOffers (both own properties) · frozen 2026-09-27

The "before" half of a real case study, recorded before the entity and
content work so the "after" has something honest to compare against.
Nothing here is projected or estimated. Every figure comes from the
`prompt_checks` table.

## The measurement

Two prompts per brand, checked by the daily `check-prompts` cron against
live models. Each check asks a buyer-style question and records whether the
brand appeared in the answer. The brand is resolved per prompt through
`company_profile_id`, so the two studies do not contaminate each other.

AEOrank
  1. "what's the best way to improve visibility in AI chatbot answers"
  2. "What's the best way to improve visibility in ChatGPT and Claude search results?"

SaaSOffers
  3. "What's the best way to get discounts on SaaS tools for my startup?"
  4. "How can I get free credits for AWS and other SaaS tools as a startup"

## Two caveats, stated up front

These limit what the follow-up can honestly claim.

**1. The model mix is lopsided.** Claude is ~81% of all checks; Perplexity is
3 checks across both brands. If the follow-up window has a different mix, a
change in the overall rate could come from the mix rather than from
visibility. Per-model rates are the only fair comparison, and Perplexity's n
is far too small to support any claim at all. Worth balancing the mix before
the follow-up window opens — but changing it mid-window is worse than
leaving it skewed.

**2. The series has a hole.** 33 measurement days across a 68-day window,
including one 35-day gap. It is not a continuous daily series.

## What has to stay fixed

The instrument cannot change mid-study or the comparison is worthless: the
same four prompts, the same scoring logic (post-`d0cab1d`, which stopped
counting "I have never heard of it" as a mention — two earlier reports scored
100 and 75 purely on that bug), and ideally a steadier model mix.

If prompts are added for other reasons, keep these four intact and compare
only these.
